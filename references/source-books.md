## Cross-Book Synthesis

The books converge on five core claims:

1. DD exists to reduce information asymmetry and convert uncertainty into price, terms, or deal-stopping issues.
2. A report should not be a pile of facts. It should answer whether the deal works, under which assumptions, and what could break it.
3. Business, financial, and industry diligence must be triangulated. Business DD forms the hypothesis, financial DD quantifies and verifies it, and industry DD benchmarks it.
4. Financial DD must test earnings quality, cash conversion, working capital pressure, tax/internal-control risk, and management projections, not just describe statements.
5. Legal DD is a risk gate for subject qualification, ownership, contracts, IP, permits, litigation, labor, tax, environmental, and transaction enforceability.

## V2 Self-Audit Expansion

After the initial skill build, the framework was expanded to cover details that are easy to miss if the books are only reduced into high-level headings:

- transaction and stage adapters: VC/early growth, growth PE, Pre-IPO, buyout/M&A, strategic investment, debt-like/buyback-heavy structures, commercial factoring/supply-chain finance, real estate/infrastructure/PPP, mining/resource rights, and overseas M&A;
- industry adapters: hard tech, semiconductor, pharma/biotech/medical device, SaaS/software/platform, manufacturing, consumer/channel, project engineering/environmental, and asset-heavy/concession models;
- valuation and terms: value/valuation/exit/risk/strategy bridge, method selection, normalized base, base/downside/upside return cases, exit feasibility, fund-life fit, and risk-to-term mapping;
- red-flag playbook: revenue authenticity, inflated profit, business packaging, related-party dependence, cash conversion stress, customer/supplier concentration, IP ownership, major contract transferability, tax/labor/environmental issues, litigation/guarantees, management integrity, unsupported forecasts, and infeasible exits;
- report-writing patterns: conclusion-first executive judgment, thesis table, risk language, scope/assumptions/limitations, IC memo compression, bottom-paper discipline, and final QA.

## V3 Noah Requirements Expansion

Noah's later testing on 或宇科技 exposed three practical requirements that are now part of the skill:

- full DD reports must preserve detailed basic information, including entity facts, history, cap table, governance, financing evolution, transaction terms, product/team/finance basics, source conflicts, and evidence gaps;
- the five source books must be fused module by module, not only cited as general inspiration;
- the default final full-report format is standalone HTML, with local parser, sensitive-content, desktop, and mobile readability checks.
