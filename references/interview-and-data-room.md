# Interview And Data Room

## Core Data Request List

Company:

- Business license, articles, shareholder register, cap table, historical equity changes.
- Organization chart, governance documents, board/shareholder resolutions.
- Company history, product list, facilities, subsidiaries, branches.
- Historical capital contributions, noncash contribution valuation/title documents, equity pledge/freeze/dispute records.
- Licenses, permits, qualifications, annual filings, prior names, restructuring or major asset transfer documents.

Financial:

- Audited reports, management accounts, trial balance, general ledger.
- Revenue detail by customer/product/channel/region/contract.
- Sales contracts, orders, invoices, delivery/acceptance documents, receivables aging, collections.
- Cost detail, supplier purchases, inventory detail, payroll, capex, fixed assets.
- Bank statements, loan agreements, guarantees, pledges, off-balance-sheet obligations.
- Tax filings, tax incentives, VAT/invoice records, tax inspections or penalties.
- Forecast model, budget, management plan, pipeline and backlog.

Business:

- BP/CIM, product materials, demos, pricing, SKU/product architecture.
- Customer list, top customer contracts, retention/churn, satisfaction, complaints.
- Supplier list, top supplier contracts, supply constraints.
- Sales pipeline and CRM exports.
- Production/service process, capacity, utilization, quality metrics.
- R&D roadmap, patents, software copyrights, technical documents.
- Product definition, product structure, working-principle materials, application cases, technical benchmark data.
- Orders, backlog, acceptance documents, project milestone records, after-sales/warranty/return data.
- Channel/distributor agreements, rebate/return policies, end-customer sell-through data where applicable.
- Inventory aging, warehouse records, production records, energy/logistics/packaging/labor data for nonfinancial cross-checks.

Legal/compliance:

- Licenses, permits, qualifications.
- Major contracts and contract ledger.
- IP certificates, license/transfer agreements, infringement disputes.
- Litigation, arbitration, administrative penalties, investigations.
- Labor contracts, social insurance, compensation, non-compete.
- Environmental, safety, quality, data/security compliance documents.
- Related-party list and transactions.
- Land/property/equipment ownership or lease documents, mortgages, pledges, encumbrances.
- Tax filings, tax incentive approvals, tax inspections, arrears/penalties, invoice practices.
- Environmental impact assessment, acceptance, monitoring reports, pollutant permits, safety-production records.
- Major contract review fields: term, rights/obligations, restrictions, transferability, termination, breach liability, dispute forum, approvals.

## Management Interview Guide

Founder/CEO:

- Why does this business exist and why now?
- What is the company's true core capability?
- Which customers would be hardest to lose and why?
- What has changed versus the BP/CIM?
- What assumptions in the forecast worry you most?
- What would make this investment fail?
- Which part of growth is product pull, channel push, policy cycle, price increase, or one-off project?
- What evidence would convince a skeptical investor that the company is not over-earning?

CFO/Finance:

- Revenue recognition policy and major judgment areas.
- Order-to-cash process.
- Receivables aging and collection discipline.
- Gross margin changes by product/customer.
- One-off items and normalization adjustments.
- Working capital and funding need.
- Tax and internal-control issues.

Sales/Customer Success:

- Customer acquisition process.
- Pipeline quality and conversion.
- Pricing and discount authority.
- Churn/lost deals and reasons.
- Customer concentration and renewal risk.
- Recent order examples: source, product, amount, gross margin, delivery, acceptance, payment status.
- End-customer visibility if revenue comes through distributors or agents.

Product/R&D/Technology:

- Product architecture and roadmap.
- Differentiation versus competitors.
- Build vs buy vs integration.
- IP ownership and technical debt.
- Delivery and implementation bottlenecks.
- Technical indicators that customers actually care about.
- Which modules are proprietary, outsourced, open-source, agency/resale, or customer-specific customization.

Operations/Supply Chain:

- Capacity, utilization, yield, quality.
- Supplier concentration and alternatives.
- Inventory practices.
- Delivery lead time and bottlenecks.
- Bottleneck process, energy/logistics/packaging/labor relationship to output, outsourcing and subcontracting.
- Slow-moving inventory, warranty/return/complaint issues, safety/environmental observations.

HR:

- Key-person dependency.
- Hiring plan and critical vacancies.
- Incentive plan.
- Labor compliance and disputes.
- Headcount by department, tenure, turnover, social insurance/housing fund, overtime, contractor/dispatch usage.
- Employee view of culture, founder style, internal controls, and whether actual operations match management's story.

## External Interview Guide

Customers:

- Why did you buy?
- What problem does the product solve?
- Who else did you evaluate?
- What would make you churn?
- Satisfaction, renewal, budget, usage, switching cost.

Suppliers:

- Purchasing volume, payment discipline, bargaining position.
- Supply constraints.
- Relationship history and dependence.

Competitors/experts:

- Market segmentation.
- Target's reputation and real position.
- Differentiation and weaknesses.
- Pricing, margins, customer behavior, regulation.

## Interview Processing Rules

- Turn each interview into claims, evidence, contradictions, and follow-up questions.
- Note whether the interviewee has incentive bias.
- Cross-check interview claims against documents and data.
- Do not quote private interview content verbatim in user-facing replies unless Noah asks and sensitivity is cleared.
